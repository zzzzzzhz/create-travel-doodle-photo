# Prompt Patterns

Use these structures as internal prompt scaffolding. Replace bracketed content with scene-specific details and keep the style block consistent.

## Text-to-image pattern

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Primary scene: [subject, action, environment].
Camera and composition: [camera angle, lens feeling, framing, aspect ratio, focal hierarchy].
Lighting and mood: [time, light direction, atmosphere].
Reality constraints: keep [faces, hands, anatomy, vehicle/product/architecture geometry, physics] believable and coherent.

Create a convincing realistic editorial photograph as the dominant base. Overlay a youthful travel-magazine handmade doodle collage at controlled medium intensity, about 31–36% graphic presence. Build one coherent directional flow from [scene-specific action or geometry]. Use selective imperfect bright-yellow marker contours, cobalt-blue and teal brush strokes, orange action marks, rough black ink arrows and dotted paths, hand-drawn stars, music notes, quotation marks, clouds and abstract symbols, plus sparse tiny magenta stickers. Place most graphics behind or beside the focal subject and only thin accents in front.

Use at most two tiny beige torn-paper scraps, each only 2.5–4% of canvas height and combined below 6%, at safe outer edges. Full bleed.

Avoid large paper, giant typography or numbers, readable added text, logos, watermark, border, white margin, pure illustration, flat poster design, malformed anatomy, duplicated subjects, and graphics covering focal details.
```

## Image-to-image pattern

```text
Edit Image 1 as the target photograph. Preserve its exact [composition, crop, aspect ratio, perspective, lighting, identities, faces, expressions, hands, anatomy, clothing, objects, animal features, vehicle geometry, architecture, existing text, numbers and signage]. Do not redraw, replace, move, or cover these details. Keep the realistic photograph dominant.

Apply a youthful travel-magazine handmade doodle collage at controlled medium intensity, about 31–36% graphic presence. Organize one connected visual flow around [the target's actual motion, gaze, frame, reflection, route, sound, steam, wind, or geometry]. Add selective imperfect yellow contours, cobalt-blue and teal brush energy behind the subject, orange action accents, rough black arrows and dotted routes, contextual stars/notes/quotation marks/clouds/abstract symbols, and sparse tiny magenta stickers. Use only a few thin foreground marks and never cover faces, hands, text, animals, products, vehicles, or key architecture.

Use no more than two tiny beige torn-paper scraps, each 2.5–4% of canvas height and combined under 6%, only at safe outer edges. No giant paper, letters, numerals, headlines, borders, or white margins. Preserve the original aspect ratio and dimensions.
```

## Adapt the graphic anchor

Choose one main anchor and one supporting anchor. Do not use every motif at equal strength.

| Scene | Main anchor | Supporting anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walking group | group silhouette and steps | crosswalk or gaze direction |
| Vehicle | road curve and wheel direction | wind or body contour |
| Mirror/window | frame orbit | reflection or exterior view |
| Speaker/music | vibration rings | waveform and music notes |
| Cooking | wok or hand arc | flame and ingredient flight |
| Sign/building | structural outline | arrows, wires, or light |
| Animal | gait, tail, paws, or gaze | environmental path |
| Quiet portrait | light, steam, or reading path | plants, window, or gaze |
| Water/nature | current, bubbles, mist, waves | terrain or horizon |

## Revision language

When a user changes intensity, revise the numeric graphic presence and affected elements explicitly:

- Lighter: 24–29% graphics; fewer broad strokes; keep contour and directional flow.
- Approved medium: 31–36% graphics; use the fixed specification.
- Stronger: 38–43% graphics; increase brush energy and contextual marks, but keep all focal details unobstructed.

When a user changes paper size, adjust only the paper rule. Do not compensate by changing every other graphic element.
